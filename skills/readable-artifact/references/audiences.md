# Audiences

Decide who the reader is before writing. Give the reader one value on each of the three axes below. The values on one axis do not overlap.

Each axis sets a different part of the page. Axis 1 sets what the page covers, axis 2 sets the words, and axis 3 sets how much background to give.

## Axis 1. What the reader does after reading

Reviewing a pull request or giving feedback on a plan is Decide.

| Value | Keep | Leave out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decide | The decision asked of them, the options with their cost, a recommendation | Step-by-step execution |
| Act | What they do and when, the steps in order | Options that were considered and dropped |
| Stay informed | What changed, and whether it changes their work | Execution detail and dropped options |

## Axis 2. What the reader knows about the field

A reader who knows only some of the terms the page uses counts as outside the field.

| Value | Words | Leave out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outside the field | Words from their own daily work | Internal names, tools |
| Inside the field | The field's own terms | Basics they use every day |

## Axis 3. Whether the reader took part in the work

A reader who followed only part of the work counts as not having taken part.

| Value | Background | Leave out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Took part | What is new since they last saw it | Background and discussion they already know |
| Did not take part | The question that arose, how it was answered, and why | Side discussions and asides |

When readers differ, choose all three axes for the reader the page exists for.

## Translating terms

- Replace a term with what the reader sees, does, or experiences as a result.
- If the reader will keep hearing the term elsewhere, introduce it once, after the plain description.
- If a sentence explains how something works and the reader only needs what happens, cut the explanation.

## Check the words

Step 5 checks these on the plan, and step 7 checks them again on the text of the built page.

- The reader would not have to ask what any noun means.
- Nothing is referred to by a number, a made-up code, or its position instead of its name.
- Where the reader needs only the outcome, no passage explains how it is built.
- No passage explains what the reader already knows.
